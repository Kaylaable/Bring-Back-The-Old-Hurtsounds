Hey, welcome to the GitHub page for Bring Back The Old Hurtsounds!

All the mod really does is add the old hurtsound back into the game, which plays once only a player entity (you or your friends) gets hurt :3

I work on this mod all by myself! I have no extra team, however, obviously this sound is NOT mine! It is technically a free sound you can use in any project, however, it is used by Mojang (the company that is owned by Microsoft that works on the game)

-----------------------------

What I'm actively working on:

- The DataPack! That means you no longer would have to install either Forge (legacy versions of this mod, unfortunately, unsupported from 23/10/26 by me), Fabric or NeoForge and can play with this mod on a completely vanilla (no mods) version of Minecraft!

- The Mod Description! Why this isn't a thing yet? Ngl, completely forgot to make one up to this point, please don't yell at me 😭

- Configs! Once I figure out how these work, I will be adding them to new builds! That means, you will be able to change how loud or quiet the sound is as well as change how often the higher pitched form of the hurtsound plays (so for example, it plays half of the time, you can choose to make it 1/3, 1/5, 1/10 or even all the time!)
